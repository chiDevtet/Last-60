# Brand

## Name exploration

Criteria: memorability, pronunciation, crypto-native appeal, brandability, logo potential,
availability. Domain status was checked against the **authoritative registry RDAP services**
(Verisign `.com`, Identity Digital `.io`, CentralNic `.xyz`, Radix `.fun`, Google `.app`) on
2026-11-05; each endpoint was validated with a known-registered control domain first. "free" means
the registry returned 404 (not registered). Social handle availability could not be verified
without authenticated X/Telegram API access and should be checked manually before launch.

| # | Name | Meaning / fit | .com | .io | .xyz | .fun | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LAST60** | the final 60 seconds decide the round | taken | **free** | **free** | **free** | describes the mechanic; numeric marks are crypto-native; language-neutral; strong logo (ring-as-zero) |
| 2 | LASTSIXTY | spelled-out variant | taken | free | free | free | longer, less punchy |
| 3 | FINALSIXTY | "final sixty" | free | free | free | free | clear, but two words and generic |
| 4 | TMINUS60 | countdown idiom | taken | free | free | free | rocket/launch cliché; hard to say |
| 5 | LASTCALL60 | "last call" | free | free | free | free | bar/alcohol connotation |
| 6 | ZEROHOUR | the decisive moment | (zerohourfi.com free) | free | free | free | widely used phrase; weak ownership |
| 7 | SIXTYROUND | rounds of sixty | free | free | free | free | flat, descriptive |
| 8 | KICKBACK60 | fee kickbacks | free | free | free | free | "kickback" reads as bribery — rejected |
| 9 | CLOSER | the buyer who closes the round | (closerfi.com taken) | free | free | free | generic word, crowded |
| 10 | CADENCE | five-minute rhythm | (cadencefi.com taken) | free | taken | free | fintech feel but generic, taken |
| 11 | FINAL ARC | the logo concept | taken | free | taken | free | good concept, weaker name |

**Decision: keep LAST60.** It is the only candidate that explains the product in five
characters, survives being spoken, and yields a distinctive mark. Primary domain recommendation:
**last60.io**, with **last60.xyz** and **last60.fun** as defensive registrations. Ticker suggestion:
`$LAST60` (or `$L60` where tickers are length-limited).

## Concept — "the final arc"

A round is a ring of five segments, one per minute. Only the last segment — the final sixty
seconds — is lit (violet → cyan), ending in a bright tick at twelve o'clock where the round closes.
The same ring is the "0" of the wordmark, so icon and wordmark are a single idea. The website's
countdown *is* the logo, animated.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#07080C` | page background |
| Carbon | `#0D1017` | surfaces, chart surface |
| Graphite | `#141924` | raised surfaces |
| Frost | `#E9EDF5` | primary text |
| Mist | `#8B93A7` | secondary text |
| Signal | `#38E1FF` | brand accent, live states, countdown |
| Pulse | `#7B61FF` | the final arc, final-minute intensity |
| Series | `#1A9FCC` | chart marks — Signal is too light for dark-mode data marks (OKLab L 0.84); Series passes the validator (lightness band, chroma, contrast) |
| Good / Warn / Bad | `#25A06C` / `#BF8A16` / `#E0565A` | status only, always with an icon + label (✓ ◷ ✕); red/amber CVD separation is in the 6–8 band, so colour is never the only cue |

Light-mode logos use `#0A0D14` text with a darker cyan→violet arc (`#0796C7` → `#5A3FEA`).

## Typography

* **Geist** — UI, headings, figures (semibold for values; proportional figures for standalone
  numbers).
* **Geist Mono** — terminal, addresses, signatures, labels; tabular figures for columns and the
  ticking clock (so digits don't jitter).

## Voice

Fintech-precise, not casino. Say *recipients*, *rewards*, *eligible fees*, *settled*. Avoid
*win*, *jackpot*, *lucky*, *bet*. Every claim on the site is something the chain can verify.

Brand guidelines are also published at `/brand` on the website.
